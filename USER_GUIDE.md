## 1. Core Philosophy & Architecture

### The Problem This System Solves
- **Dual-Track Friction**: Trying to study both MERN and Govt Exam simultaneously leads to decision paralysis (*"What should I study right now?"*).
- **Commute & Energy Dips**: Fixed weekday travel windows (`8:15–9:30 AM` & `6:00–7:00 PM`) require seamless transitions.
- **Forgetting Curve**: Studying concepts once and forgetting them 10 days later.
- **Motivation Fluctuations**: Days with low energy or high fatigue risking broken streaks.

### The Science-Backed Solutions
- **Circadian Energy Matching**: High-focus analytical tasks (DSA / Mock Tests) are scheduled during morning cortisol peaks; lecture watching and conceptual reviews are scheduled during mid-day post-lunch windows.
- **Spaced Repetition Active Recall**: 3-day and 7-day automated memory consolidation prompts.
- **Zero-Distraction Audio**: Pure browser Web Audio 40Hz Gamma beats and brown noise.
- **Anti-Laziness Micro-Sprints**: 15-minute low-friction sessions that preserve daily momentum.

## 5. AI Daily Copilot & Smart Decision Hub

Located prominently in the main dashboard (`SmartCopilotAdvisor.jsx`), this is your **interactive, adaptive AI decision companion**.

### What It Does:
You can ask **any real-time question, state, or dilemma** (such as *"I want to sleep for 1 hour right now"*, *"I have 30m free before leaving"*, *"My brain is fried from coding"*, *"I missed my morning session"*), and the engine analyzes:
1. Your real-time clock and current circadian energy window.
2. Today's logged minutes across MERN vs. Govt exam tracks.
3. Your sleep targets and remaining daily slots.
4. Cognitive load mechanics (e.g. sleep inertia physics, prefrontal working memory burnout).

### Key Features:
- **Natural Language Query Bar**: Type your exact state in plain language.
- **Quick Daily Dilemma Chips**: 1-click preset queries for:
  - 💤 *Want to sleep for 1 hour* (Gives 25m Power Nap vs 90m Full REM Cycle advice to avoid 60m sleep inertia).
  - ⚡ *Only 30m free right now* (Recommends high-yield rapid drills / 1 DSA logic question).
  - 🧠 *Brain is fried / Mentally exhausted* (Switches to low-friction story lectures or Anti-Boredom analogies).
  - ⏰ *Missed morning session* (Compresses schedule without guilt and shifts remaining blocks).
  - ⚖️ *MERN vs Govt balance check* (Checks logged minutes and points to whichever track is falling behind).
  - 🎲 *Too lazy to start* (Activates 2-Minute Rule dopamine micro-wins).
- **Integrated Nap & Focus Live Timer**:
  - In-card MM:SS countdown with presets (`15m`, `25m`, `60m`, `90m`).
  - Integrated Web Audio soundscapes: 🌧️ Gentle Rain, 🟫 Brown Noise, 🧠 40Hz Gamma Beats.
  - Automatic completion chime audio.
- **1-Click Interactive Actions**:
  - `[ ⏱️ Start Nap/Focus Timer ]`: Runs countdown immediately with audio.
  - `[ 🔄 Shift Timetable (+1h / +30m) ]`: Automatically reschedules remaining timetable tasks.
  - `[ 💻 Jump to MERN ]` / `[ 🏛️ Jump to Govt ]`: Automatically switches tabs and scrolls smoothly to track roadmaps.
  - `[ 🥊 Launch 60s Drill ]` & `[ 💡 Open Anti-Boredom Vault ]`.

## 6. Quick Study Logger & Auto-Tick Engine

The heart of your daily tracking system (`StudySessionLogger.jsx`).

### Step-by-Step Workflow:
1. **Select Track**:
   - **`💻 MERN & DSA Track`** *(Indigo)*: Automatically loads all Striver DSA Steps & MERN Modules.
   - **`🏛️ Govt Exam Track`** *(Terra)*: Automatically loads Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, GS, English, and Current Affairs.
   - **`✨ Life, Cleaning & Self-Care`** *(Teal)*: Bedroom cleaning, washroom cleaning, book reading, educational podcasts, workouts, pooja/meditation.
2. **Choose Module & Topic**:
   - Select from the dropdown or use the **Quick Search** input (e.g. typing `Binary Search` or `Room Cleaning`).
   - Or click **`🎲 Too Lazy? Pick 1 Micro-Topic`** for an instant randomized 15-minute challenge.
3. **Select Duration & Mode**:
   - Choose a preset: `15 min (Micro)`, `25 min (Pomodoro)`, `45 min`, `60 min`, `90 min`, or type custom minutes.
   - Or click **`15m Sprint Timer`** or **`Live Stopwatch`** to time your work in real time.
4. **Select Time Mode**:
   - **`Now (Real-Time)`**: Automatically stamps the current clock time.
   - **`Past Time Preset / Custom`**: Easily pick past times (e.g. `11:30 AM`, `2:15 PM`) if you studied earlier and are logging now.
5. **Retention Difficulty Rating**:
   - Select `🔴 Tough (Review in 3d)`, `🟡 Moderate`, or `🟢 Mastered`.
6. **Submit & Auto-Tick**:
   - Click **`Log Mins & Auto-Tick Topic Progress`**.
   - **What happens automatically**:
     1. The topic is marked completed (`✅`) in your master syllabus roadmap.
     2. Today's study minutes and habit checkboxes are updated in MongoDB and LocalStorage.
     3. A **Smart Post-Session Advisor** appears with Step 1 (rest timer) and Step 2 (next subject suggestion).
